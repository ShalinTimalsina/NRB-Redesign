    Key Functions
    • Secretariat duties including NRB Board and Management Committee,
    • Media handling, publication and dissemination of information to stakeholders,
    • Managing international communication and coordination with foreign central banks, financial institutions/agencies and other relevant entities as well as organizing high-level international seminars, meetings, and conferences,
    • Monitoring and analyzing global economic and financial developments and their possible implications,
    • Dealing technical cooperation (MoU and TA) with other central banks, international organizations and training institutes.
