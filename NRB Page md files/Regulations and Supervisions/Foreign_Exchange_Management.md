Key Functions

Formulate and implement foreign exchange policies,

Manage foreign exchange reserve and to determine the system of foreign exchange rate,

Issue license, regulate, inspect and supervise the institutions wishing to transact in foreign exchange,

Formulate policies, provide approval, keep record and manage repatriation of foreign direct investment and foreign debts,

Formulate policies to facilitate the import and export of goods and services.
