In UI/UX design, this specific component is generally called a **"Local Navigation"** or **"Sub-navigation"** (often abbreviated as "Sub-nav"). 



Because we made it stay at the top of the screen when you scroll past it, the full technical term for this specific implementation is a **"Sticky Local Navigation Strip"** or a **"Sticky Tab Bar"**.



### What to ask for in the future:

If you ever want this implemented in another section of the website (like Statistics or Publications), you can use any of these clear prompts:



* *"Can you add a **sticky local navigation strip** to the [Section Name] pages?"*

* *"Please implement a **sub-nav bar** so I can switch between the child pages without going back."*

* *"Add a **sticky tab menu** to group these related pages together under one parent."*



Using the terms **"Sticky"** (so I know to make it stay on scroll) and **"Local Navigation"** (so I know it applies only to that specific subsection, unlike the global header) will instantly tell me exactly what you need!
