Department Overview

The Assets and Service Management Department is responsible for the procurement and management of goods, services, and infrastructure required for the operations of Nepal Rastra Bank. The department ensures transparent, competitive, and fair procurement processes in accordance with the Public Procurement Act and related regulations.
