Board of Directors

The Board of Directors (BOD) of Nepal Rastra Bank comprises seven members. Among those, four are ex-officio members — including the Governor who serves as Chairperson — and three are non-executive Directors. All members are appointed by the Government of Nepal, Council of Ministers for a five-year term as per the NRB Act, 2002.
