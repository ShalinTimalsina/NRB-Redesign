The Central Bank of Nepal

Nepal Rastra Bank

Nepal Rastra Bank is the central bank of Nepal and the country's monetary authority. Established in 1956, NRB is responsible for maintaining monetary and financial stability, formulating monetary and foreign exchange policies, promoting a secure payment system, and supporting sustainable economic development.

Who We Are

Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) was established in 1956 as the central bank of Nepal. The Bank plays a vital role in maintaining macroeconomic stability, supervising and regulating the financial sector, managing foreign exchange reserves, developing payment systems, and fostering public confidence in the financial system.

Central Headquarters Baluwatar, Kathmandu

Nepal Rastra Bank Central Office

Formulated under the Nepal Rastra Bank Act 1955 (now Act 2002) as the supreme monetary & regulatory authority of the Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal.
