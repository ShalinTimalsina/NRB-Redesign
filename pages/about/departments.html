Departments, Divisions & Units

Nepal Rastra Bank is organized into specialized departments, each supporting a core function of the central bank. Each department is further structured into divisions and units.

Hierarchy

The structure follows a three-tier model: Department → Division → Unit. Each tier has clearly defined roles and reporting lines.
