Regulatory Framework

Regulation & Supervision

Nepal Rastra Bank ensures a stable, secure, and transparent financial system by providing robust regulatory frameworks and continuous supervision of all licensed banks, financial institutions, and payment service providers.

Our Regulatory Mandate

Under the Nepal Rastra Bank Act, the central bank holds exclusive authority to issue licenses, formulate prudential regulations, and conduct on-site and off-site supervision to mitigate systemic risks and protect depositors' interests.

Risk Management

Risk-Based Supervision (RBS)

Nepal Rastra Bank is actively transitioning to a Risk-Based Supervision framework to proactively identify and mitigate institutional risks.

  • Continuous off-site monitoring
  • Targeted on-site inspections
  • Early warning systems
