Monetary Policy

Nepal Rastra Bank formulates and implements monetary policy to maintain price stability and support sustainable economic growth. The Bank uses a range of instruments — including open market operations, standing facilities, and reserve requirements — to manage liquidity and keep inflation within its target band.

Policy Mandate

Under the Nepal Rastra Bank Act, 2002, the Bank is mandated to maintain price stability, manage liquidity in the financial system, and support the Government's broader economic development objectives through appropriate monetary measures.

Current Policy Rates FY 2083/84

Policy Rate: 6.50%

The Bank Rate and Standing Liquidity Facility rate remain unchanged from the previous review, reflecting a measured stance amid moderating inflation.
